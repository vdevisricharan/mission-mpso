## 3. Scheme of Examination (Annexure-II)

The written examination consists of **2 objective-type papers**, conducted either via Computer Based Recruitment Test (CBRT) or OMR offline mode.

| Paper | Subject | Questions | Duration | Maximum Marks | Medium |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper-I** | General Studies and General Abilities | 150 | 150 Minutes | 150 | Bilingual (Telugu & English) |
| **Paper-II** | Statistics (Common for all) (Degree Level) | 150 | 150 Minutes | 150 | Bilingual (Telugu & English) |
| **Total** | | **300** | **300 Minutes** | **300** | |

## 5. Minimum Qualifying Marks (Section 12.2)

- **OC, Sportspersons & EWS:** Not less than **40%** (120 / 300 marks)
- **BCs:** Not less than **35%** (105 / 300 marks)
- **SCs, STs & PHs:** Not less than **30%** (90 / 300 marks)

*Note: As explicitly stated in the notification, securing minimum qualifying marks does not vest any right in a candidate for selection; selection is purely based on relative merit and zonal vacancy roster.*
